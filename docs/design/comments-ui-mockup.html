Comment and decision UI mock-up

Three looks for each of the two reader controls on a Lotuspod page: the comment thread at the end of a section, and the decision question card. Every state is drawn side by side and nothing here is wired up; the real comment box under each section is today's UI, for comparison.

Settled: decision cards A. Each thread style now opens with a long conversation, drawn in that style, above its gallery of states.

The fixes common to all three: a comment's status is a badge in its header line, never a line under the text; a waiting thread says the page is checking and the reply appears in place without a reload; before an answer, Reply is quieter and reads as adding detail; a saved decision folds to one line with a way to change it.

Comment threads: A

Inline. Stays in the prose: no card, a hairline between threads, the status as an outlined pill on the header line, an agent's reply indented under a lavender rule. The place the reply will land is held by a dashed rule while the page checks.

Decision cards: A

✓ Chosen

The maintainer picked this style for decision cards. B and C stay below for reference, folded.

Inline. A question in the prose's own voice with a quiet rule down its left edge. Options are a plain list of themed radio rows; the default carries a muted "default" after its label. The rule turns lavender while an answer is unsaved, and a saved answer folds to one line.

Decision cards: B

Cards with segmented pills. Each question is a raised card with the question in the display serif. The options are one segmented pill control; a small dot marks the default. The footer holds the note and the Save button, and a saved card keeps its question and shows the answer as a tinted band with a Change button. Not chosen; kept for reference.

Show decision cards B (not chosen)

Decision cards: C

Compact inline control. One question is one row: the question on the left and its choices as small chips beside it, wrapping underneath when the row is short of room. Choosing a chip shows Save and a note link on a second line; a saved question shrinks to a single dashed row. Pairs with the chat thread, where a decision can sit in the conversation like a quick reply. Not chosen; kept for reference.

Which one?

Decision cards are settled on A. What remains is the thread style: A, B or C, or a mix of parts, for example B's card with A's thread line, or A's thread with C's typing bubble. Compare the long thread at the top of each section, say which in this section's comment box, and name anything that should change before it is built.

Two behaviours are drawn the same way in all three thread styles, so say if either is wrong:

  • Before an agent answers you can still add to your comment; the link reads Add to your comment and is quieter, and becomes Reply once there is an answer. The other choice is to hide it until an answer arrives.
  • While a thread is waiting, the page checks every few seconds and drops the reply into place without a reload; once nothing is waiting, it stops checking.

My suggestion: threads A. Its thread line keeps a long conversation readable without nesting deeper, it reads as part of the page like the chosen decision cards, and its status sits clearly in the header line.
